Recipe Cart TikTok maker

A week of 7 photo posts: 6 meal and recipe carousels with no store named (STOP hooks, takeout swaps, too tired dinners, POV, dinners worth coming home to, one meal plus its recipe) and 1 Kroger edition. Every meal photo is followed by its recipe slide, and the last slide points to tryrecipecart.com for more meals and recipes. Make slides, save them, post.

Tip: post one a day. Slide 1 does best with a real food or pantry photo; text only is the fallback. If you use AI photos, turn on TikTok's AI-generated label when you post. Save the slides, upload them to TikTok as a photo post, and paste the caption.

TikTok bio options (tap to copy)

Name field: Recipe Cart | Meal Ideas. Pick one bio and keep it.

Brand spine and real app runs

Promise: never eat the same thing twice. Proofs: variety, any fridge, cost. Every post uses one proof, and sharing is the growth loop.

Cost posts only show dollar amounts from runs you paste here, one per line: Meal title | item price, item price. Example: Chicken rice bowl | chicken 6.20, rice 1.80, broccoli 2.10. With no runs, cost posts show no prices. Keep it to a few runs you actually post, not a price log.

Header library

Formula (from Lazy Meal Prep): a big hook with one or two CAPS words and one emoji, then a small bracket line: (With recipes), (Kroger edition), (Couple edition), I've got you or Swipe for recipes >>>. Covers are one of three photos: a grocery haul packed edge to edge, one hero dish cropped by the frame, or meal prep containers. Text sits in the middle, white with a thin black outline, no box, no logo or watermark on photo slides. Lead with broad promises (dinners from what you already have) and use audience editions second. Cover tip: tap Add photo on the first slide and use a real photo of your own fridge or groceries, or Generate photo for an overhead grocery photo. On the In the app slide, Add photo replaces the drawn screen with a real screenshot.
